1. ORGANISATION CYBERSECURITY POLICY

*Purpose:* To protect the organisation's data, systems, and people from cyber threats, data breaches, and misuse.

*Scope:* Applies to all staff, contractors, vendors, and anyone using company devices, networks, email, and data.

*Policy Owner:* [IT Manager / CISO]

2. CORE PRINCIPLES
1. Confidentiality, Integrity, and Availability of all information.
2. Least Privilege - users get only the access they need.
3. Zero Trust - verify everything, trust nothing by default.

3. KEY POLICY AREAS

*A. Acceptable Use*
- Company systems are for business use only.
- No illegal downloads, streaming pirated content, or accessing offensive material.
- No bypassing security controls (VPN, firewall, antivirus).

*B. Password & Authentication*
- Minimum 12 characters, mix of upper, lower, number, symbol.
- Must use Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) for email, VPN, and admin systems.
- No sharing passwords. Use approved password manager [e.g., 1Password, Bitwarden].
- Passwords expire every 90 days for privileged accounts.

*C. Device & Endpoint Security*
- All laptops/phones must have antivirus, disk encryption, and auto-lock after 5 mins.
- Auto-updates must be enabled. No personal USB drives without scan.
- Lost/stolen devices must be reported within 2 hours.

*D. Email & Phishing*
- Verify sender before clicking links or attachments.
- Report suspicious emails to [security@company.com].
- IT will run quarterly phishing simulations.

*E. Data Protection & Classification*
- *Confidential:* Customer data, financials, passwords - encrypted, access-controlled.
- *Internal:* Policies, internal memos - staff only.
- *Public:* Website content.
- No customer data on personal devices or personal Gmail/WhatsApp.

*F. Network & Internet*
- Firewall and secure Wi-Fi (WPA3) required. No public Wi-Fi without company VPN.
- Guest Wi-Fi must be separate from internal network.

*G. Access Control*
- Access granted based on job role, approved by HOD.
- Access revoked within 24 hours of staff exit.
- Quarterly access review for all critical systems.

*H. Backup & Recovery*
- Daily automated backup of critical systems.
- Backups encrypted and stored offsite/cloud.
- Restoration test done quarterly.

*I. Incident Response*
If you suspect a breach:
1. Disconnect from network but DO NOT turn off the device.
2. Report immediately to [IT Helpdesk / +234...].
3. Do not attempt to delete or fix it yourself.
IT Team must respond within 1 hour and document incident.

4. ROLES & RESPONSIBILITIES
- *Management:* Approve policy, provide resources.
- *IT/Security Team:* Enforce, monitor, train, and update.
- *All Staff:* Follow policy, report incidents, complete security training.

5. TRAINING
All staff must complete cybersecurity awareness training at onboarding and annually.

6. VIOLATIONS
Violation may lead to disciplinary action, up to termination and legal action, depending on severity.
